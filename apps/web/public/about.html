About Chasa

Why this exists

Most invoice tools help you send the invoice. Almost none help with the awkward part after — the follow-up when it's late and you're stuck between sounding pushy and sounding like you don't mind waiting. Chasa writes that email for you, tone-matched to how overdue it is.

What it isn't

Chasa doesn't send email on your behalf, connect to your accounting software, or chase clients automatically. You paste invoices, get a draft, and send it from your own inbox. Clients hear from you — not from a collections bot.

Who's behind it
