The problem

The invoice is 30 days overdue. The client hasn't replied to the first reminder, if you even sent one. You know you need to follow up from your own Gmail — clients trust an email from you, not an automated system — but starting from a blank compose window is where most follow-ups die.

The manual way

Open Gmail, hit compose, and write the follow-up from scratch — trying to sound firmer than the last email without sounding annoyed. Then remember to check for a reply.

The Chasa way

Connect Gmail once in your Chasa workspace. Chasa drafts a tone-matched follow-up — firmer as it gets more overdue — and saves it directly into your Gmail Drafts folder. It can also read client replies to suggest what to say next. You always review and hit send yourself; Chasa never sends on your behalf.
