What Chasa is

Chasa is a tool that drafts payment follow-up emails from invoice details you provide. The free tier includes a limited number of AI drafts per month; a paid plan removes that limit.

You send the email

Chasa does not email your clients. Drafts are for you to copy, edit, and send from your own inbox. You're responsible for what you send.

No guarantees

The service is provided as-is, with no uptime guarantee and no warranty that a draft will get you paid or suit every situation. AI output can be wrong — review before sending.

Acceptable use

Don't use Chasa to generate abusive, fraudulent, or unlawful content. We may restrict access that violates this without notice.

Billing

Paid plans are billed through Stripe. Cancel anytime from your account; access continues through the end of the billing period already paid.
