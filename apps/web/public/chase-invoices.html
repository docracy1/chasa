Chase invoices — without the awkward email

Chasing payment is part of freelancing. Chasa handles the wording so you can focus on the relationship — paste invoices, get drafts, send from your inbox.

Chase, don't collect

Chasa is built for freelancers who want to get paid without sounding like a collections agency. We draft the email; you decide when and how to send it. Clients always hear from you, not from us.

A simple chase workflow

Add overdue invoices to Chasa (type them in, upload CSV, or sync from QuickBooks/Xero on paid plans). Review the draft, tweak tone with Soften or Firm up on Solo+, then copy to your email client. Mark done when paid.
