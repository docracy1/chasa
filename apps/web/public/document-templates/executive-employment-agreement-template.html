Executive Employment Agreement

Free executive employment agreement template covering compensation, equity, severance, and restrictive covenants for a senior executive hire.

Drafting a executive employment agreement from scratch is slow, and generic templates often miss the sections that actually matter. This free template is built for founders, freelancers, and small business owners formalizing a business decision or agreement without paying a lawyer to draft it from scratch. It's structured with the standard sections a document like this needs, with clearly marked [placeholder] fields so you can fill in your own details quickly. Copy it into your own word processor, fill in the placeholders, and review it — or adapt it — before use. It's part of docstoc's free document template library, alongside business, legal, real estate, finance, and HR templates. Every template here is free to copy with no signup required, and each one carries a plain disclaimer: this is a starting point for informational purposes, not a substitute for advice from a licensed professional in your jurisdiction.

Executive Employment Agreement

Company: [Company Name] · Executive: [Name] Position: [Title] · Effective Date: [Date]

1. Duties

Executive will serve as [Title], reporting to [Board/CEO], with duties consistent with that role.

2. Compensation

Base salary: [Amount] per year · Annual bonus target: [%/Amount], based on [Criteria] Equity: [Number] options/RSUs, vesting over [Schedule].

3. Benefits

Executive is eligible for [Company Name]'s standard benefits plans, plus: [Any executive-specific perks].

4. Term and Termination

[ ] At-will employment. [ ] Fixed term of [Duration], renewable by mutual agreement.

5. Severance

If terminated without Cause, Executive will receive: [Severance terms — e.g. months of salary, COBRA coverage, accelerated vesting].

6. Restrictive Covenants

Executive agrees to [confidentiality / non-compete for [Period] where enforceable / non-solicitation of employees and customers for [Period]].

7. Change of Control

[Any provisions triggered by a company sale or merger — e.g. accelerated vesting.]

Company: ______________________ Date: ____________ Executive: ______________________ Date: ____________

*This document is provided for informational and educational purposes only and does not constitute legal advice. Non-compete enforceability varies enormously by state (some, like California, ban them almost entirely) — have an employment attorney review the restrictive covenants section against the specific state law that will govern this agreement.*
