Free estoppel certificate template — a tenant, borrower, or party to an agreement confirms the current status of that agreement, commonly required before a property sale, refinance, or lease assignment.

Estoppel Certificate

Certifying Party: [Tenant/Borrower Name] · Requesting Party: [Landlord/Lender/Buyer Name] · Underlying Agreement: [Lease/Loan, dated [Date]] · Date: [Date]

Certification

The undersigned certifies the following to be true and accurate as of the date of this certificate:

1. The [Lease/Loan Agreement] referenced above is in full force and effect, and has not been modified except as follows: [List any modifications, or "none"]. 2. As of this date, [Landlord/Lender] is not in default under the agreement, to the best of the undersigned's knowledge, except: [List, or "none"]. 3. The current [monthly rent / loan balance] is [Amount], paid through [Date], with no offsets, credits, or claims against amounts owed, except: [List, or "none"]. 4. The [lease term expires / loan matures] on [Date], with [Number/no] renewal options remaining. 5. No advance rent or additional security deposit has been paid beyond [Amount], currently held by [Landlord]. 6. The undersigned has no known claims or disputes against [Landlord/Lender] related to this agreement, except: [List, or "none"].

Reliance

This certificate is provided with the understanding that [Requesting Party/a prospective buyer or lender] will rely on it in connection with [a pending sale/refinance/assignment] of the property.

Certifying Party signature: ______________________ Date: ____________

*An estoppel certificate is legally significant precisely because it "estops" (prevents) the signer from later contradicting what they certified — a tenant who certifies no landlord default exists generally can't turn around afterward and claim otherwise against a buyer who relied on that certificate to close.*
