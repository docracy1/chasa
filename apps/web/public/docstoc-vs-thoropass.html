docstoc vs Thoropass — auditor-paired compliance vs. automated evidence

Thoropass pairs its compliance software with an in-house audit team, so companies going through SOC2 or ISO27001 get consultants and auditors bundled with the platform — a genuinely different, service-heavy model, priced on a custom, quote-based basis. docstoc doesn't sell audits or consulting hours at all. It's a free, AI-drafted invoice-chase tool from a small EU team, with transparent flat pricing from $14.99/mo, and it's making a different bet: automation over consulting. That bet is already live for accounts receivable — every chase docstoc drafts is timestamped and logged into an audit trail auditors can use today (see SOX evidence automation). Extending that same automated-evidence model to the kind of general SaaS evidence Thoropass's auditors help you gather manually is on our roadmap, not something we're claiming to have shipped.

FAQ

Is docstoc a Thoropass alternative?

Not today, and not for the same job. Thoropass bundles compliance software with an in-house audit team, so you go through your SOC2 or ISO27001 audit with consultants and auditors on tap. docstoc doesn't offer audit or consulting services at all — it's a free AI invoice-chase tool that already automates the evidence trail for accounts-receivable activity, with a roadmap toward broader automated evidence collection instead of adding consulting hours.

How much does Thoropass cost compared to docstoc?

Thoropass prices on a custom, quote-based basis that reflects the software plus the audit/consulting services bundled in — check thoropass.com directly for a current quote. docstoc publishes transparent flat pricing starting at $9/mo for Solo, with no consulting fees because it doesn't sell consulting.

Does docstoc provide auditors or compliance consulting like Thoropass?

No. docstoc doesn't pair customers with auditors or sell compliance consulting, and it isn't SOC2 or ISO27001 certified today — see the honest roadmap at /security. What docstoc is betting on is automation instead of consulting hours: a live, timestamped AR/invoice audit trail today (see /use-cases/sox-evidence-automation), extending to broader SaaS evidence automation as it's built.
