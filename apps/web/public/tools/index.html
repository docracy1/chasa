Small, no-signup utilities that match docstoc's products: finding the right template, checking a file's hash, tracking SSL expiry, verifying corporate domain trust badges, and estimating what late payments cost.

Template finder

Pick your situation, get a direct link to the right free business or legal template.

File hash checker

Why these tools

Most tools here run entirely in your browser — no signup, no data upload. The trust-badge lookup only fetches a public profile you already have a link for. Use them to size a decision, then do the actual work in docstoc if it's a fit.
