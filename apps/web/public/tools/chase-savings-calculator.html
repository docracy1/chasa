How this ROI estimate works

Every day an invoice sits unpaid ties up cash. If your average unpaid balance is high and days outstanding are long, even a modest reduction frees working capital you can use for payroll, tax, or growth.

Chasa does not auto-email clients. It writes the follow-ups so you actually send them — the missing step for most freelancers who “mean to chase later.”

FAQs

How does this chase savings calculator estimate cash unlocked?

It divides your unpaid AR balance by current average days outstanding to estimate daily cash tied up, then multiplies by the days you expect to shorten with consistent follow-ups.

Is this the same as a DSO or ROI calculator?

It is a practical DSO / cash-flow style estimate plus time savings. It is not tax, accounting, or investment advice — use it to size the cost of slow collections.

How does Chasa help reduce days outstanding?

Chasa drafts tone-matched reminders for each overdue stage so you follow up sooner and more consistently. You still send the emails yourself.
