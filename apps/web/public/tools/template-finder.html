FAQs

Do I need an account to use these templates?

No. Every template is free to view and copy without signing up — an account only matters if you want to submit your own template or use docstoc's other tools.

What if my situation isn't listed?

The eight situations above are the most common starting points, not the full list. Browse all 488+ templates for anything more specific — business, legal, real estate, finance, and HR documents are all covered.

Can I edit the template after copying it?

Yes. Templates are plain text you copy into your own document — there's no lock-in format or proprietary editor involved.
