Client Payment Risk Scoring Automation

Stop waiting 60 days to realize a client isn't paying. Chasa automatically evaluates historical payment trends, overdue ratios, and promised payment reliability to generate an instant risk score.

Why Client Risk Scoring Matters

  • Early Detection: Spot high-risk clients before delivering additional work or issuing new invoices.
  • Adaptive AI Escalation: Automatically trigger earlier, firmer follow-up drafts for high-risk accounts.
  • Promised Date Tracking: Monitor clients who repeatedly break payment promises and auto-adjust their risk tier.
  • Seamless Accounting Integration: Syncs directly with QuickBooks Online and Xero accounts.
