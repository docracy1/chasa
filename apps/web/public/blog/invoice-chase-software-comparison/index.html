FAQ

Is Chasa a Chaser alternative?

Yes for freelancers and small teams who want cheaper, draft-only follow-ups. Chaser targets SMB/mid-market AR with auto-send starting at a much higher price point.

Does Chasa auto-send payment reminders?

No. Chasa writes the email; you copy it into Gmail, Outlook, or Apple Mail (or open a mailto link). Clients always hear from you.

How does Chasa pricing compare?

Solo is $7/mo flat per workspace; Pro is $17/mo. Competitors often use revenue tiers, seat caps, or higher entry plans (Paidnice from $69/mo, Chaser Compact from $259/mo).

Can Chasa replace Paidnice or Duefy?

If you need full auto-send sequences and a hosted payment portal, those tools may fit better. If you want AI drafts, tone controls, and inbox-first sending at a lower price, Chasa is built for that.
