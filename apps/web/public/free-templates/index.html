FAQ

Are these invoice templates really free?

Yes — all templates are free to view, copy, and edit with no account or signup required. Chasa never emails your clients for you; you copy the draft into your own inbox.

Can I edit these templates?

Yes. Copy the subject and body, then swap in your invoice number, amount, due date, and client name before sending from Gmail, Outlook, or Apple Mail.

Which template should I use for an overdue invoice?

Match the tone to how late it is: a gentle nudge at 1–3 days overdue, firmer at 7–14 days, and a formal notice from 30 days onward. Use the category filter above to jump straight to the right stage.

Is there a way to get a version matched to my exact invoice?

Yes — the free AI tool drafts a tone-matched follow-up from your actual invoice details (client, amount, due date) instead of a generic template.
