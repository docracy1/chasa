Boilerplate

docstoc is a free AI tool that writes payment reminder emails for freelancers and small businesses. Upload a CSV or enter invoices manually — docstoc drafts a follow-up for each one, tone-matched to how many days overdue it is (friendly at 1–7 days, firmer at 30+). Users review and send from their own inbox; docstoc never sends on their behalf. Built by RELACON GmbH in Vienna, Austria.
