Compare docstoc

docstoc shows up in two very different conversations, and we'd rather point you to the right one than blur them together. First: freelancers and small teams picking between AI-drafted, draft-only invoice chasing (docstoc) and the auto-send or credit-control tools below — that's a straightforward, apples-to-apples comparison on price and features. Second: teams evaluating GRC platforms like Vanta and Drata for SOC2/ISO27001. docstoc is not a GRC platform today. What we've actually built is an evidence-automation feature — live now for AR and invoice-chase audit trails — and we're extending that model to broader SaaS evidence as a roadmap item, not a shipped feature. See exactly what's live versus planned on our security page.

SSL / TLS certificate providers

docstoc's Pro plan includes free, automated SSL for a customer's own domain — real Let's Encrypt certificates, issued and renewed automatically. Here's how that compares to dedicated certificate authorities and resellers.

  • docstoc vs ZeroSSL — both free and ACME-based; docstoc needs no separate cert dashboard.
  • docstoc vs Let's Encrypt — the CA docstoc's automation is actually built on.
  • docstoc vs Sectigo — free automated DV vs $70–460+/yr paid certs.
  • docstoc vs DigiCert — small-business DV vs enterprise OV/EV and PKI.
  • docstoc vs Cloudflare SSL — no DNS-proxy re-architecture required.
  • docstoc vs SSL.com — both free at the DV tier; docstoc needs no separate ACME setup.
  • docstoc vs GlobalSign — free single-domain automation vs paid enterprise PKI.
  • docstoc vs SSL For Free — automatic renewal vs a manual ~90-day cycle.
  • docstoc vs FreeSSL.org — a disclosed Let's Encrypt backend vs an undisclosed CA.
  • docstoc vs Certum — free and automatic vs a cheap but manually-renewed paid cert.
  • docstoc vs GoDaddy SSL — no renewal price ever, vs widely-reported renewal price hikes.
